# Anshul Travel Agencies – Customer & Business Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

Anshul Travel Agencies Customer & Business Analytics is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding customer behavior, travel preferences, purchase patterns, and overall business performance.

The project uses **SQL, Excel, Power BI, and Python** to transform raw travel purchase data into meaningful business insights and decision-support reports.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze travel agency customer and purchase data to identify:

- Customer travel behavior and preferences
- Purchase patterns and trends
- Customer segments
- Key business KPIs
- Revenue and business performance indicators
- Opportunities for business growth
- Areas where customer engagement can be improved

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses a travel purchase analysis dataset containing customer and travel-related information.

The dataset is used to analyze:

- Customer characteristics
- Travel preferences
- Purchase behavior
- Travel plans
- Customer segments
- Business performance

The original dataset is available in the [`data`](./data/) folder.

## 🔍 Analytics Approach

### 1. Data Preparation

The dataset was reviewed and prepared for analysis to ensure that the data could be effectively used for business reporting and analytics.

### 2. SQL Analysis

SQL was used to perform business-oriented analysis such as:

- Customer-level analysis
- Purchase analysis
- Aggregations
- Segmentation
- Trend analysis
- KPI calculations
- Business performance analysis

### 3. Excel Analysis

Excel was used for supporting data exploration, calculations and analytical reporting.

### 4. Power BI Dashboard

Power BI was used to create interactive business dashboards and reports for visualizing important metrics, customer behavior and business insights.

### 5. Python Analysis

Python was used as an additional analytics tool for data exploration and supporting analysis.
