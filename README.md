## 📌 Project Overview

**Anshul Travel Agencies – Customer & Business Analytics** is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding customer behavior, travel preferences, purchase patterns, customer segments, and business performance.

The project transforms raw travel purchase data into structured analysis, interactive dashboards, business reports, and actionable insights using:

- SQL
- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Python
- Business Intelligence techniques

The primary goal is to move from **raw data to meaningful business decisions** through a structured analytics workflow.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

Anshul Travel Agencies has access to customer and travel purchase data, but raw data alone does not provide clear visibility into customer behavior, travel preferences, purchasing patterns, and business performance.

The key business challenge is to transform the available raw data into **meaningful, measurable, and actionable insights** that can support better decision-making.

The analysis focuses on understanding:

- Who are the major customer groups?
- What travel preferences can be identified?
- How do customers behave across different travel plans?
- What purchasing patterns exist within the customer base?
- Which customer segments can be targeted more effectively?
- What business KPIs should be monitored?
- What patterns may indicate potential growth opportunities?
- How can customer and purchase insights support better business decisions?

This project addresses these questions through data analysis using **SQL, Excel, Power BI, and Python**, followed by business-focused reporting and recommendations.
